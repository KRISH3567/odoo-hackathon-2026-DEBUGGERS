1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Background & Purpose:
Traditional micro, small, and medium businesses (MSMEs) rely heavily on manual 
paper registers, fragmented Excel spreadsheets, and informal communication channels 
to manage warehouse inventory. This causes frequent stockouts, untracked shrinkage, 
delayed customer shipments, and costly discrepancies.

1.2 The Goal:
Build a modular, web-based Inventory Management System (IMS) — "StockSense" — 
that digitizes and unifies all incoming, outgoing, internal, and adjustment stock 
operations into an intuitive, real-time platform matching Odoo's world-class 
double-entry inventory philosophy.

1.3 Target Users & Personas:
  - Inventory Managers: Manage vendor receipts, customer dispatch orders, stock 
    valuations, safety stock rules, and high-level warehouse reporting.
  - Warehouse Staff: Handle daily physical operations — barcode scanning, shelf 
    picking, packing, internal bin transfers, and cycle counts.

3. CORE MODULE SPECIFICATIONS (OFFICIAL REQUIREMENTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Authentication & Security:
  - User Sign-Up and Log-In flow with role attribution (Manager / Staff).
  - Simulated OTP-based password reset ("Forgot Password" -> 6-digit OTP verification).
  - Instant redirection to the main Inventory Dashboard upon authentication.

3.2 Landing Dashboard & Executive KPIs:
  The dashboard renders a live snapshot of warehouse throughput.
  - Top 5 Required KPI Metric Cards:
      1. Total Products in Stock (Total on-hand SKU quantity and valuation in INR).
      2. Low Stock / Out of Stock Items (Count of SKUs at or below safety stock).
      3. Pending Receipts (Incoming vendor purchase orders awaiting arrival/validation).
      4. Pending Deliveries (Outgoing customer sales orders awaiting pick/pack/validation).
      5. Internal Transfers Scheduled (Transfers queued between internal storage locations).
  - Dynamic Filters Toolbar:
      • By Document Type: Receipts | Delivery Orders | Internal Transfers | Adjustments
      • By Status: Draft | Waiting | Ready | Done | Canceled
      • By Warehouse / Location: All | Main Warehouse | Production Floor | Rack A | Rack B
      • By Product Category: Raw Materials | Finished Goods | Electronics | Grocery

3.3 Product Management:
  - Create, view, and update products with the following schema:
      * Product Name (e.g., "Steel Rods", "Office Chairs", "Wireless Mouse")
      * SKU / Internal Reference Code (e.g., "RAW-STL-001", "FURN-CHR-010")
      * Category (Raw Material, Work-in-Progress, Finished Goods, Consumables)
      * Unit of Measure (UoM: kg, units, meters, liters, boxes)
      * Cost Price & Selling Price
      * Initial On-Hand Stock (optional on creation)
  - Real-Time Multi-Location Stock Availability:
      * Breaks down quantity on hand across Warehouse 1, Main Store, Production Rack, 
        and Delivery Staging.

3.4 Operation 1: Receipts (Incoming Stock Flow):
  - Purpose: Register and validate incoming goods received from suppliers.
  - Step-by-Step Flow:
      1. Click "New Receipt".
      2. Select Supplier and Destination Warehouse/Location.
      3. Add product line items and quantity received.
      4. Document moves from "Draft" -> "Ready".
      5. Click "Validate": System automatically increments product stock, marks 
         the status as "Done", and records a credit in the Stock Ledger.
      * Example: Receive 50 units of "Steel Rods" -> Total stock immediately increases by +50.

3.5 Operation 2: Delivery Orders (Outgoing Stock Flow):
  - Purpose: Manage picking, packing, and dispatch of finished goods to customers.
  - Step-by-Step Flow:
      1. Click "New Delivery Order".
      2. Step 1 - Pick Items: Warehouse staff verifies items picked from racks.
      3. Step 2 - Pack Items: Items verified and packaged for shipping.
      4. Step 3 - Validate: System automatically decrements stock, marks status 
         as "Done", and logs a debit in the Stock Ledger.
      * Example: Customer order for 10 Chairs -> Delivery order reduces chairs by 10.

3.6 Operation 3: Internal Transfers:
  - Purpose: Relocate materials and inventory within internal company premises.
  - Step-by-Step Flow:
      1. Select Source Location (e.g., "Main Store" or "Rack A").
      2. Select Destination Location (e.g., "Production Rack" or "Rack B").
      3. Specify product and quantity.
      4. Click "Validate Transfer":
         * Total company-wide inventory remains completely unchanged.
         * Location-level breakdown updates instantly (Main Store -20, Production Rack +20).
         * Every movement is permanently logged in the Move History ledger.

3.7 Operation 4: Inventory Adjustments (Physical Cycle Counting):
  - Purpose: Resolve mismatches between system recorded quantities and physical counts.
  - Step-by-Step Flow:
      1. Select Product and Location to audit.
      2. Display current System Recorded Stock.
      3. Enter actual Physical Counted Quantity.
      4. System automatically computes:
         * Variance = Physical Count - System Count
         * Shrinkage % = ((System - Physical) / System) * 100
      5. Select Adjustment Reason (e.g., "Damaged Items", "Cycle Count Discrepancy", "Theft").
      6. Click "Apply Adjustment": System auto-updates recorded stock to match physical 
         count and writes an immutable audit record in the Stock Ledger.

3.8 Move History (The Double-Entry Stock Ledger):
  - The single source of truth for all inventory movements.
  - Table Schema:
      * Reference ID (e.g., "WH/IN/0001", "WH/INT/0002", "WH/OUT/0003")
      * Timestamp (Date & Time)
      * Product Name & SKU
      * From (Source Location / Vendor)
      * To (Destination Location / Customer / Scrap)
      * Quantity & UoM (+50 units, -10 units)
      * Movement Status (Done)


4. 🏆 "FEATURES TO WIN" (UNIQUE INNOVATIONS TO BEAT ALL COMPETITORS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To stand out and secure 1st place in the Odoo hackathon, StockSense implements 
five high-impact, enterprise-grade capabilities that other submissions will lack:

4.1 Innovation 1: Interactive "1-Click Official Odoo Scenario Walkthrough"
  - A prominent demo banner in the top navigation: "▶ Run Official Odoo Flow".
  - One-click executes the exact 4-step example provided in Odoo's problem statement:
      * Step 1: Receive 100 kg Steel from Vendor (+100 in Main Store).
      * Step 2: Internal Transfer: Move 80 kg from Main Store -> Production Rack.
      * Step 3: Deliver Finished Goods: Ship 20 kg to Customer (-20 stock).
      * Step 4: Adjust Damaged Items: 3 kg damaged found on rack -> adjust stock (-3).
  - Evaluators watch the KPIs, Location quantities, and Stock Ledger update live 
    in front of their eyes within 15 seconds.

4.2 Innovation 2: Odoo Double-Entry Visual Flow Map
  - An interactive visual node diagram showing real-time inventory moving between:
    [Vendors (Virtual)] ---> [Main Store] ---> [Production Floor] ---> [Customers (Virtual)]
                                   │
                                   └───> [Scrap / Damaged (Virtual)]
  - Evaluators will recognize Odoo's signature double-entry methodology visualized cleanly.

4.3 Innovation 3: Dynamic Predictive Reordering Rules (AI Stress-Test)
  - Instead of static min/max numbers, StockSense introduces an intelligent formula:
      Reorder Point (ROP) = (Average Daily Demand * Lead Time) + Safety Stock
      Days of Inventory Remaining (DIR) = Current Stock / Average Daily Demand
  - Includes interactive "Demand Spike" (0% to +100%) and "Supplier Delay" sliders.
  - An evaluator can slide to "+50% demand" and see low-stock alerts trigger immediately 
    with a 1-click "Create Draft Receipt" button.

4.4 Innovation 4: Smart Barcode & Quick-Action Scanner Simulator
  - Built specifically for the "Warehouse Staff" persona.
  - A virtual scanner tool where users can click or scan SKU barcodes to trigger instant:
      * Instant Pick validation
      * Quick Bin-to-Bin Transfer
      * Instant Count verification
  - Features audio chime confirmation and visual scanner beam animations.

4.5 Innovation 5: FEFO & Perishable Batch Expiry Intelligence
  - Tracks lot/batch numbers with expiration dates for perishable and consumable goods.
  - Implements First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) picking priority over simple FIFO.
  - Recommends automated progressive dynamic markdowns (15% -> 35% -> 60%) for 
    batches nearing expiry to salvage margin before spoilage.

4.6 Innovation 6: Shrinkage Financial Impact & Anomaly Detection
  - Turns simple stock adjustments into actionable business intelligence.
  - Automatically calculates total rupee value of damaged and lost inventory.
  - Highlights highest-risk leakage categories and generates a downloadable Audit Summary.
